Priya Soneji

Hi! I’m a Bay Area–based mechanical engineer with a passion for working on challenging, meaningful problems that have a tangible benefit to people and the world around us.

I fell in love with engineering at an early age through Lego Mindstorms and Arduino, which quickly turned into years of building random gizmos, robots, and mechanical systems. That curiosity eventually grew into a broader interest in using engineering to solve difficult real-world problems. I thrive in fast-paced environments alongside close-knit, diverse, driven, and passionate teams, where ideas can move quickly from a rough concept to something designed, built, tested, and deployed in the real world. I especially enjoy multidisciplinary work that sits at the intersection of mechanical design, robotics, manufacturing, controls, and field engineering.

I’m currently a founding engineer at Terranova, where I’m developing a network of terraforming robots designed to create new habitable land, restore degraded habitats, and protect coastlines and critical infrastructure from flooding at scale.

Outside of any one project, I’m driven by the challenge of building ambitious systems that push the limits of what is practical and can have a lasting positive impact.

Feel free to reach out through LinkedIn with any inquiries, collaborations, or interesting problems worth solving.
